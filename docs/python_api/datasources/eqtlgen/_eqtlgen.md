---
title: eQTLGen single-cell
---

The [eQTLGen consortium](https://www.eqtlgen.org/) single-cell project meta-analyses single-cell RNA-seq of peripheral blood mononuclear cells across cohorts, and fine-maps the associations with SuSiE.

We ingest two datasets: the cis-eQTLs of 6 cell types (one study per cell type and gene) and the co-expression QTLs of 5 cell types (one study per cell type and gene pair). Both have the study type `sceqtl`.
