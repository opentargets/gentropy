---
title: Pathway enrichment
---

GO biological process and Reactome gene sets built from an Open Targets platform release, and a logistic score test of their enrichment among the genes of each disease, adjusted for gene length, protein-coding TSS density and the number of pathways of each gene, with saddlepoint p-values. Used by the pathway enrichment L2G features.
